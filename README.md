# Privacy-on-Wheels-paper

Privacy on Wheels: Bystander Surveillance, Data Protection, and Property Rights in Connected Vehicles 

Repository for the academic paper exploring the legal, regulatory, and property rights challenges associated with connected vehicle sensors, cameras, and bystander surveillance in the European Union.

## Abstract & Overview

Modern connected vehicles are equipped with extensive sensor arrays and cameras (such as Tesla's Sentry Mode) capable of capturing continuous footage of public spaces. This paper investigates the legal friction between vehicle-based monitoring and privacy rights, addressing:

- **Bystander Surveillance:** The unintended capture of data from pedestrians and third parties in public and semi-public spaces.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Obligations under the GDPR and the EDPB Guidelines 01/2020 on processing personal data in the context of connected vehicles.
- **Data Access & Property Rights:** The intersection of industrial property rights, software patents, and vehicle-generated data access under the recent EU Data Act.
- **Case Studies:** Analysis of regulatory interventions (such as European data protection authority probes into Tesla) and industry responses.
