## 1. Official European Union Documents and Guidelines

### EDPB (European Data Protection Board) – Guidelines 01/2020 on Connected Vehicles

* **Why use it:** It is the reference regulatory text for the application of the GDPR within the automotive ecosystem. It defines which vehicle data constitutes personal data (including that of passersby and pedestrians), the applicability of Art. 6 GDPR, and clarifies the non-negotiable boundaries of the household exemption (Art. 2(2)(c) GDPR) when cameras record public space.
* **Official Link:** [EDPB Guidelines 01/2020 on Processing Personal Data in Connected Vehicles and Mobility Related Applications (PDF)](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files_en?file=consultation/edpb_guidelines_202001_connectedvehicles.pdf)

### EU Data Act Regulation (EU Data Act - Regulation EU 2023/2854)

* **Why use it:** Crucial for the section on *Property Rights*. It regulates data ownership, access, and sharing for data generated by connected products and related services (including autonomous vehicles). It balances the user's right to access generated data with the protection of trade secrets and OEMs' intellectual property.
* **Official Link:** [Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 - EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R2854)
* **Industry Analysis:** [CUPRA EU Data Act - Rights to Vehicle Data & Transparency](https://www.cupra.com/en/eu-data-act)

## 2. Case Studies: Vehicle Video Surveillance (Tesla Sentry Mode)

### Investigation and Decision by the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Dutch DPA - Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)

* **Why use it:** Demonstrates the concrete enforcement of the GDPR by a European supervisory authority against a major automaker. The investigation focused on automated video surveillance of passersby. The proceedings were closed after Tesla implemented *Privacy by Design* software modifications (headlight visual alerts, reduction of recording duration and range).
* **Legal Analysis (Pinsent Masons):** [Tesla car security cameras 'privacy-friendly' after Dutch watchdog probe](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/tesla-car-security-cameras-privacy-friendly-dutch-watchdog-probe)
* **Industry Overview (Teslarati):** [Tesla avoids fine from Dutch watchdog after Sentry Mode changes](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-evades-dutch-watchdog-fines-sentry-mode-camera-changes/)

### German Court Ruling and VZBV Legal Action (Federation of German Consumer Organisations)

* **Why use it:** Highlights the legal liability of the individual vehicle owner/user. Rulings and warnings from German authorities clarify that operating *Sentry Mode* in public spaces exposes the vehicle owner to direct risks of fines for GDPR and personality rights violations.
* **In-depth Article:** [Autoevolution - Tesla Sentry Mode Faces Problems in Europe (Public Filming Constraints)](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/tesla-sentry-mode-faces-problems-in-europe-where-filming-in-public-places-is-prohibited-213082.html)

## 3. Industrial Property, Software Patents, and Vehicle Data

### Official Position of ACEA (European Automobile Manufacturers' Association)

* **Why use it:** Provides the European automotive industry's perspective on the EDPB guidelines. It enables the paper to analyze friction points between strict privacy enforcement, technical data security requirements for autonomous driving, and the protection of intellectual property in onboard software.
* **Position Paper (PDF):** [ACEA Comments on EDPB Guidelines 01/2020](https://www.acea.auto/files/ACEA_comments_EDPB_guidelines_1-2020.pdf)
